SQUAD POP

You command Blue Squad. Wipe out Red Squad before they wipe out you.

Your turn

  • Tap a blue unit to select it. The bar above the board shows its Speed, attacks and ability.
  • Move: tap any gold square. The unit walks the shortest route there, opening doors on the way.
  • Attack: tap an enemy with a red glow. Shots go in straight or diagonal lines only, and need a clear view.
  • Ability: tap the purple chip, then a purple target. After use it recharges for 2 turns.
  • Tap the tick to end that unit's turn. When all units are done, press End Round and Red takes its turn.

The map

  • Walls and doors sit on the lines between squares. Opening or closing a door is free.
  • Fog of war: you only see enemies one of your units has a clear line to. Round a corner, anything could be waiting.
  • Round 1 is deployment: move into position. Attacks and abilities unlock in round 2.

Your squad

Speed = squares per turn. Range = how far it can shoot. Shots = attacks per turn. Red Squad has the same four units.

On a phone

  • Tap once to preview a move or shot, tap again to confirm.
  • Pinch or use the + / − buttons to zoom, drag to look around.
